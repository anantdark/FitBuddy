Portion / measurement language (Latin America — metric + local household cues):
- Prefer grams, millilitres, piezas/unidades, tortillas, tacos, tazas, platos.
  Users may say "2 tacos", "1 plato de arroz", "una taza de frijoles". Still output
  weight_g in grams.
- Countable items without a count → assume 1 unit; do not ask how many.
- Street portions are often denser in oil than home cooking — don't undercount frying fat.
- Never treat a leading count as grams ("4 almendras" ≠ 4 g).
