One flow. Real food.

Capture → review → watch the day add up. No calorie spreadsheet required.

Built for how you actually eat.

Dal, roti, leftovers, hotel thalis — freeform text and photos both work.

  • Smart logging

    Photo or loose text. Your chosen AI estimates calories and macros — including Indian dishes.

  • Meal review

    Edit the dish name, tweak ingredient weights, watch macros update live. Save presets for next time.

  • Workouts that stick

    Structured sessions with sets and reps, or upgrade a simple AI log into a full workout.

  • Private by default

    Logs live on-device. Encrypted backups optional. Bring your own AI key — OpenRouter, Gemini, OpenAI, or Ollama.

Three steps.

  1. 01

    Install

    F-Droid, OpenAPK, or the GitHub APK. Android 10+.

  2. 02

    Connect AI (or skip)

    OpenRouter sign-in, API key, or offline simulator — your call.

  3. 03

    Log today’s food

    Snap a plate or type “2 roti + dal”. Review, save, done.
